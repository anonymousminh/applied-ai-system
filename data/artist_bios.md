# Artist Bios

## Lila Vance
Lila Vance is an indie pop and jazz-pop singer-songwriter known for bright,
sun-soaked vocals and approachable melodies. Her work spans upbeat city-pop
("Sunrise City") and lounge-style swing ("Sunday Swing"), making her a versatile
choice for listeners who want warmth and optimism.

## Neon Avenue
Neon Avenue is a synth-pop duo focused on driving, dance-floor energy. Their
production leans on punchy drum machines and bright synth leads. They are most
loved for high-energy commute and workout playlists.

## Max Power
Max Power is a high-BPM pop producer specializing in workout and motivation
tracks. Songs are designed for sustained energy above 0.9 with steady tempos
near 140 BPM. Best for runners, lifters, and high-intensity training.

## Soft Static
Soft Static is a lofi project blending warm tape hiss, mellow keys, and
unhurried percussion. The duo records on analog gear, giving every track a
calm, rainy-window feel suited to study, focus, or evening wind-down sessions.

## Slow Tide
Slow Tide is a lofi solo artist whose work leans melancholic and introspective.
Their tracks are often used for journaling, late-night reading, and quiet
emotional reflection rather than focused study.

## Iron Echo
Iron Echo is a hard-rock band known for cinematic, high-intensity tracks with
heavy guitar tones and driving drums. Their sound sits between modern rock and
post-metal, popular for competitive gaming and high-stakes workouts.

## Black Mile
Black Mile plays alt-rock with a melancholic, road-trip-after-dark aesthetic.
Their songs typically combine mid-tempo grooves with reflective lyrics, ideal
for solo drives and emotional decompression.

## Pulse Theory
Pulse Theory produces melodic electronic music ranging from upbeat club tracks
("Galaxy Threads") to calm ambient pieces ("Deep Ocean Dub"). They are often
recommended for users who want one artist that can flex across moods.

## Bit Forge
Bit Forge crafts dark, intense electronic music with industrial influences.
Their tracks favor minor keys, aggressive synths, and high-energy builds, often
chosen by listeners who want focus-with-edge for coding or design sprints.

## June Hollow
June Hollow is a folk songwriter whose work centers on acoustic guitar and
intimate vocals. Songs lean melancholic and reflective, popular among readers,
writers, and listeners seeking quiet emotional grounding.

## Coastal Birds
Coastal Birds is a folk trio with a brighter, harmony-rich sound. Their music
feels like coastal sunshine and is well suited for laid-back social settings,
brunches, and easy weekend listening.

## Ash Quartet
Ash Quartet is a contemporary jazz group known for smoky, slow-burning
arrangements. Their work blends classic jazz instrumentation with subtle
modern production, fitting late-night lounges and reflective dinners.
